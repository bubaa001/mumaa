I wish I could be beside you today, watching you celebrate everything you have earned and trying not to look more emotional than you. I have missed you so much. The lonely days at school were yours to get through, but you were never far from my thoughts.

You are clever, wise, and so beautifully particular about who you let close. You make me want to learn more, do more, and be a better man. You have helped raise me into someone I am proud to be, even if you do not see it that way.
